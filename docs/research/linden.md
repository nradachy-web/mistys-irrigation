# Linden, MI: irrigation research

Researched 2026-11-10. Every fact below was read on the page given beside it. Facts marked
**NOT VERIFIED** were looked for and could not be confirmed on a page I read; they stay out
of the copy. Derived numbers (my arithmetic on a cited figure) are marked "derived".

## 1. Place

- L1. Linden is a city in southern Genesee County, surrounded by Fenton Township; 2020
  population 4,142; total area 2.43 sq mi; 1,790 housing units in 2020.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linden,_Michigan
- L2. "The cities of Fenton and Linden were formed from areas formerly within the township"
  (Fenton Township). Village of Linden incorporated 1871 from the center of the township;
  became a city in 1988. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fenton_Township,_Michigan and
  https://www.lindenmi.us/about-linden
- L3. Argentine Township lies west of Linden: "The cities of Fenton and Linden are located to the
  east." The Linden Community Schools campus is in the eastern side of Argentine Township.
  Argentine Township Master Plan (adopted Jan 30, 2023), PDF p. 15 and p. 36:
  https://webgen1files1.revize.com/argentinetwpmi/Argentine%20Township%20Master%20Plan.pdf
- L4. Argentine Township ZIP codes include "48451 (Linden)", so a Linden mailing address can
  be a township home. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_Township,_Michigan
- L5. Downtown sits where Broad Street, Bridge Street and the Shiawassee River meet. "Outside the
  city limits, Broad Street is named Silver Lake Road, while Bridge Street is named Linden Road."
  City of Linden Master Plan (adopted Feb 13, 2023), PDF p. 19:
  https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/Linden%20Master%20Plan%20ADOPTED%202-13-23.pdf
- L6. Drive from Fenton: about 10 minutes (src/data/cities.ts, and the assignment brief).

## 2. Water supply

### City of Linden (in town)
- W1. "The county connection to the City of Linden water system was completed on April 29, 2025."
  "This connection changed our municipal water system from a groundwater supply to a surface
  water supply." https://lindenmi.us/projects
- W2. Pressure: "residents should start noticing an increase in water pressure." "Based on
  current data, system pressures have increased 7-12 psi throughout the community." "The city
  plans to continue making incremental increases until satisfactory pressures are reached."
  https://lindenmi.us/projects
- W3. Hardness: the change "will significantly reduce the mineral content 'hardness' of the water"
  and "should eliminate the need for private water softeners in most cases." Residents advised
  to put softeners in bypass. https://lindenmi.us/projects
- W4. 2025 Water Quality Report: "Your water came from Genesee County after May 1st, 2025 and
  before that it came from 4 groundwater wells, each over 180ft deep." City contact 810.735.7980.
  https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/2025+CCR+final+6.18.2026.pdf
  (linked from https://www.lindenmi.us/public-works)
- W5. Master Plan goal: "Connect to the Genesee County water system to alleviate long-standing
  pressure and water capacity issues." City of Linden Master Plan, Community Services goal (PDF p. 59)
  and Implementation Matrix (PDF p. 91):
  https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/Linden%20Master%20Plan%20ADOPTED%202-13-23.pdf
- W6. DPW system: four community wells, two water towers, one treatment plant, about 18 miles of
  mains, 1,500 service meters, 244 hydrants; DPW does "Cross connection inspection and prevention".
  https://www.lindenmi.us/public-works
- W7. Lawn meter (City of Linden Summer 2026 newsletter): "If you use a lot of water to irrigate
  the lawn or garden ... consider installing a lawn meter! The separate meter, registers outside
  water usage. Since the water does not enter the sanitary sewer system, you will be billed for
  water usage only." Contact the Treasurer's Department.
  https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/SUMMER+2026+%281%29.pdf
  (linked from https://www.lindenmi.us/fall-newsletter-2026). The city fee schedule lists a
  "Lawn water meter" fee and a "Base Fee for lawn water acct" (prices: never in copy).
  https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/City+Fee+Schedule+FY27+Spreadsheet-updated+11.10.2026.pdf
- W8. Cross-connection program (City of Linden with HydroCorp): the inspector does a visual
  inspection of water uses outside the home, including the "lawn irrigation system", and "will
  also walk around the perimeter of the house to observe exterior hose connections and (if
  applicable) lawn irrigation system backflow preventers." "Is this inspection mandatory? Yes."
  "If you are the owner of the home being inspected, you are responsible for any needed repairs."
  "Vacuum breaker should not be subject to continuous pressure ... (This type of irrigation set
  up/system would need to be corrected.)" https://watercustomer.com/linden/
  (The same page states testing intervals for irrigation backflow assemblies. Misty's testing
  is NOT confirmed, so testing stays out of the copy entirely.)
- W9. Hydrant flushing: May 4 to May 9, 2026 (archived city notice:
  https://web.archive.org/web/20260421093343/https://www.lindenmi.us/public-notice-of-hydrant-flushing-monday-may-4-saturday-may-9-2026)
  and October 5 to October 10, 2026, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. Fall 2026 newsletter: "During flushing,
  water may become cloudy, brown, or reddish due to stirred-up silt & iron. The water is still
  safe for use." Run cold taps until clear.
  https://irp.cdn-website.com/0b76c777/files/uploaded/FALL+2026.pdf
- W10. City sewer mains feed the Genesee County system (City Master Plan PDF p. 54). Same PDF as W5.

## 3. Iron in well water

- I1. U.S. Geological Survey, Water-Supply Paper 1499-E (1963), a water resources study whose
  stated scope is "all 644 square miles of Genesee County." "The water from the drift
  aquifer is hard or very hard and commonly contains objectionable amounts of iron." Glossary:
  "On exposure to air, iron in ground water oxidizes to reddish-brown sediment. More than about
  0.3 ppm stains laundry and utensils reddish brown."
  https://pubs.usgs.gov/wsp/1499e/report.pdf (record: https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/wsp1499E)
  Note: an old report. Copy calls it "an older USGS study of Genesee County groundwater" and
  never names the report title (it contains a city name the site does not use).
- I2. Michigan DHHS: "Iron is a naturally occurring metal in soil and rocks and some areas in
  Michigan have naturally high levels of iron in groundwater." Private well users are
  responsible for testing their own water.
  https://www.michigan.gov/mdhhs/safety-injury-prev/environmental-health/environmental-health-at-home/water
- I3. EGLE lists iron among "Standard water quality concerns for private wells".
  https://www.michigan.gov/egle/about/organization/drinking-water-and-environmental-health/water-well-construction/private-well-testing
- **NOT VERIFIED**: any Linden-area or Argentine Township iron measurement, or a Genesee County
  Health Department statement on iron. Copy says iron is common in county groundwater (I1) and
  tells well owners to watch for orange staining; it gives no local iron number.

## 5. Soils and terrain

- S1. USDA NRCS Soil Data Access (the SSURGO database behind Web Soil Survey), point queries run
  2026-11-10 at https://sdmdataaccess.sc.egov.usda.gov/Tabular/post.rest :
  - Downtown (42.8164, -83.7822): MoB, Wawasee loam, 2 to 6 percent slopes, well drained.
  - North side (42.826, -83.783): MoC, Wawasee loam, 6 to 12 percent slopes, well drained.
  - South side (42.808, -83.784): MoB, Wawasee loam, well drained.
  - West side (42.815, -83.795): BrD, Boyer loamy sand, 12 to 18 percent slopes, well drained.
  - East side (42.818, -83.770): BrB, Boyer loamy sand, 2 to 6 percent slopes, well drained.
  - Near Byram Lake (42.809, -83.790): BrD, Boyer loamy sand, 12 to 18 percent slopes.
  - Near Lake Ponemah (42.820, -83.744) and Lobdell Lake east shore (42.785, -83.818): Wawasee loam.
- S2. Wawasee series: "very deep, well drained soils formed in till"; "Permeability is moderate."
  https://soilseries.sc.egov.usda.gov/OSD_Docs/W/WAWASEE.html
- S3. Boyer series: "very deep, well drained soils formed in sandy and loamy drift underlain by
  sand or gravelly sand outwash at depths of 51 to 102 cm (20 to 40 inches)"; "Permeability is
  moderately rapid in the solum and very rapid in the substratum."
  https://soilseries.sc.egov.usda.gov/OSD_Docs/B/BOYER.html
- S4. City Master Plan soil map legend lists, within the city map: Boyer loamy sand, Wawasee loam,
  Miami loam, Oakville fine sand, Carlisle and Linwood mucks, among others (PDF p. 39, URL as L5).
- S5. Topography: "generally flat"; elevations from about 860 to 920 feet; lowest "in the central
  and western areas of the city and along the Linden Mill Pond and the Shiawassee River"; hills in
  the northeastern and southeastern parts of the city. City Master Plan PDF p. 35 (URL as L5).
- Copy uses: a mix of loam and loamy sand, both well drained; sand drains fast. No claim that any
  one street is sand or loam.

## 6. Housing and lots

- H1. "Older, historic neighborhoods are found immediately adjacent to downtown Linden," including
  homes on Bridge and Broad Streets and on Main, Oak, Mill, Tickner, Hickory, Maple, Lindenwood,
  East, South, Franklin, Aldrich and Bush. "Many newer neighborhoods are found beyond the core
  neighborhoods and within the outskirts of the city. These include the Spring Meadows, Forest
  Ridge, Saddlebrook, Creekwood, Pine Valley, and Chestnut Grove neighborhoods." City Master Plan
  PDF p. 43 (URL as L5).
- H2. Of 1,618 housing units (2019 data): 34.8% built 1980 to 2000 (largest share); 21.5% built
  2000 or later; "more than 20% of the city's housing stock was built in 1959 or earlier."
  75.2% are one-unit detached. City Master Plan PDF pp. 27-28.
- H3. "Near full build-out of several subdivisions which were started in the early 2000's
  including Forest Ridge Glens and Saddlebrook." City Master Plan PDF p. 15.
- H4. "mature street trees and neighborhood trees are prevalent throughout the city's
  neighborhoods." City Master Plan PDF pp. 18, 35.
- H5. Golf courses inside the city: Spring Meadows Golf Course/Country Club and Shiawassee Shores
  Golf Course. City Master Plan PDF p. 46.
- H6. Argentine Township Rural Residential: "Lot areas should be at least one acre, although many
  existing lots in these areas may be less than one acre." (Argentine Master Plan PDF p. 66.)
- **NOT VERIFIED**: whether the 1980s to 2000s subdivisions were built with sprinkler systems.
