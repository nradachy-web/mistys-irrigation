## House rules for numbers
- Watering: about an inch a week in summer, rain included (general guidance). Spray heads about
  1.5 in/hr, rotors about 0.5, rotary nozzles about 0.4 (typical published rates).
- Startup window: about mid April through May, once hard freezes are over.
- Blowout window: late September into November, before the ground freezes.
- Lateral pipes usually sit 6 to 12 inches down.
